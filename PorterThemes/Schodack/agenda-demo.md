# Buggy Lab 🚗

<div class='columns-2-1'>
<div class='steps'>

### Procedure

1. Mark a start line and measure a 2.0 m track
2. Time your buggy over each 0.5 m interval
3. Repeat three trials for **each** buggy
4. Graph both buggies on the *same* axes

</div>
<div>

<div class='materials'>

**Materials**
- 2 buggies
- Meter stick, stopwatch
- Whiteboard + markers

</div>

<div class='safety'>

⚠️ **Safety:** keep cords off the floor.

</div>
</div>
</div>

<div class='note'>

**Question:** Does your buggy move in a *consistent* manner?

</div>
