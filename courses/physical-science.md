# Physical Science

<span class="course-badge badge-regents">High School Level</span>

An introductory science course exploring fundamental concepts in chemistry and physics, designed to build scientific literacy and problem-solving skills.

## 📚 Course Topics

### Chemistry Fundamentals
- Matter and its properties
- Atomic structure and the periodic table
- Chemical bonding and reactions
- Acids, bases, and pH

### Physics Fundamentals
- Motion and forces
- Energy and work
- Heat and temperature
- Electricity and magnetism

### Scientific Method
- Experimental design
- Data collection and analysis
- Laboratory safety
- Scientific communication
