NOTES 1, top half. 8 minutes. MISCONCEPTION: that the planets formed at different times, or that the Sun "captured" them. One disk, one event. LINK BACK to Unit 1: this nebula is the leftover material from earlier stars - that is where the iron and the rock came from.

NOTES 1, diagram: students label crust, mantle, outer core, inner core. TRY IT 1 - why is the core iron and nickel? Two parts required: Earth was molten, AND materials sorted by density. A response with only "iron is heavy" is incomplete. TRY IT 2 - accretion: grains collide and stick, growing bodies pull in more. WORKED EXAMPLE on the page: inner planets rocky because it was too hot near the young Sun for ices and gas to collect.

NOTES 2, first sitting (meeting 4, after the dating lab). 15 minutes, to the end of this slide and the next. MISCONCEPTION: that after two half-lives the parent is all gone. It halves each time: 100, 50, 25, 12.5. MISCONCEPTION: that heat or pressure changes the rate. Nothing does - that is why it works as a clock.

NOTES 2. Have the ESSRT open to "Geologically Important Radioactive Elements Used for Radiometric Dating" - students copy the three half-lives from it. THE TRAP (Try It 4): a 200,000-year-old bone. Nearly everyone says carbon-14. Its range ends at 70,000 years. Potassium-40 is the one whose range includes 200,000.

NOTES 2, second sitting (meeting 5). 15 minutes. THE REVERSAL to land: three different kinds of sample, from three different places, give the same age. That is the evidence for one formation event. TRY IT 4 answer: 200,000 years is outside carbon-14's useful dating range (100-70,000).

NOTES 3, 20 minutes, the day after the Role of Water lab. THE MISCONCEPTION THIS WHOLE LESSON TURNS ON: "Earth was hit less." It was not - if anything more, with its stronger gravity. Ask the room before revealing the table.

NOTES 3. Tie each word to yesterday's lab: chalk in vinegar = chemical weathering; the stream table = erosion and deposition. TRY IT 5: one process AND how it removes a crater - both required. TRY IT 6: Mars has channels and 300,000 craters - water flowed in the past, but not for long or not recently. WORKED EXAMPLE on the page: the Moon has no atmosphere, water or moving plates, so nothing wears its craters away.

NOTES 4, 25 minutes, opening meeting 8. This is ADDED content: New Visions teaches radiometric dating only, and the ESSRT geologic history chart is unusable without it. It leads straight into the L2 timeline card sort that follows in the same block.

NOTES 4. Ask for the order BEFORE revealing the line under the diagram. TRY IT 7: oldest rock is Layer 1, by superposition. Both the layer and the principle are required. COMMON ERROR: placing X last because it "goes through everything". It stops at the unconformity.
